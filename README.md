# BLA2

Software Processes & Models W/ Real-World Scenarios

Project Overview
This project showcases the differences in software development models and processes and how they are able to help companies create, test, and maintain software. It also goes on to explain why having an organized development process is important for a company and system and how multiple differences could be used depending on the requirements of the project. The overall main goal is to be able to understand how software engineering works beyond the code and, more importantly, the organization of it all and what methods or models are used to get there with real-world concepts.

Real-World Applications Researched

1. EpicCare EHR — Epic Systems (Healthcare)
One real-world example used is EpicCare, which is an electronic health record system that is used by multiple healthcare organizations in order to be able to manage patient information and help medical professionals be able to accomplish their work. The development of this system takes careful planning, testing, integration, and continuous maintenance. Based on the research I’ve done, its development practices demonstrate some characteristics of a mixed/hybrid approach that combines structured planning and continuous integration.

2. League of Legends — Riot Games (Gaming)
League of Legends is an online multiplayer game developed by Riot Games. Its development involves frequent updates, automated testing, continuous integration, and player feedback. These practices demonstrate characteristics of Agile and incremental development, allowing the company to regularly improve the game.
	League of Legends, otherwise known as LoL, is an online multiplayer game that is developed by Riot Games. The development of this game requires frequent updates, automated testing, continuous integration, and player feedback. All of these listed characteristics point to an Agile and incremental development, allowing the company to regularly improve the game while users play and give frequent feedback.

Comparison of the Two Applications
The comparison shows how different types of software require different development priorities.
EpicCare focuses heavily on reliability, patient safety, documentation, and regulatory requirements. LoL cares more about frequent updates to improve gameplay for players and player feedback to give players a game they know they will love and enjoy. Although both of these applications require long planning, testing, and maintenance, both of their development approaches are extremely different because their users have different needs.
